01 — About me

Designer's eye.
Developer's brain.

I'm Dav, a self-driven creative at the start of an exciting journey. I sit right where design meets code — which means I don't just make things look good, I make them work beautifully in the browser.

I design user-centred interfaces in Figma, craft visuals and brand assets in Adobe Photoshop, and bring them to life with semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, JavaScript and React. Every project is a chance for me to learn something new and push my craft further.

As an IT Officer Specialist, I also understand the technology that keeps a business running — setting up and troubleshooting computers, networks and software, and helping people get the most out of their tools.

I'm looking for my first real-world opportunities — freelance projects, internships or a junior role — where I can add value, learn from great people and grow fast.

  2. 02

    Map user flows and structure the layout before adding any colour.

    Design

    Craft high-fidelity UI and visuals in Figma & Photoshop, then refine with feedback.

  4. 04

    Build it with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React — responsive and accessible.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Test across devices, polish the details and ship it to the world.
